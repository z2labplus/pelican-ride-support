Data in the game

This policy covers Pelican Ride, the native iPhone and iPad game published by yushen zhang. The game works offline, requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. It does not send gameplay data to developer servers.

Ride progress, achievements, language and other preferences are saved on your device to restore your journey and settings. System backups may include these files according to your device settings. The game does not provide account sync or developer-operated cloud saves.

Permissions and deletion

The game does not request location, camera, microphone or contacts access. Sound and haptic feedback can be disabled in Settings. Deleting the app and its data removes its local saves. Manage device backups through your system settings.

Purchases and support

Apple processes purchases. We do not receive full payment card details. If you contact telegram2ai@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, to respond to and investigate your request. Send only information necessary to resolve the issue. You can request deletion of personal information in support correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable retention obligations.

External links and this website

Support and privacy links open only when you choose them. This website contains no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages, the website host, may process access logs for operation and security. Apple's store, system backup and email services follow their respective privacy policies.

Children and updates

The game does not actively collect children's personal information. Avoid including unnecessary information about children in support messages. If data handling changes, we will update this policy and its date. For privacy questions, contact telegram2ai@gmail.com.

游戏中的数据

本政策适用于由 yushen zhang 发布的原生 iPhone 与 iPad 游戏《鹈鹕骑自行车》（Pelican Ride）。游戏离线运行，无需账号，不含广告、统计分析 SDK 或跨应用追踪，也不向开发者服务器发送游戏数据。

骑行进度、成就、语言和其他偏好保存在设备上，用于恢复旅程和设置。系统备份可能依照设备设置包含这些文件。游戏不提供账号同步或开发者运营的云存档。

权限与删除

游戏不请求定位、相机、麦克风或通讯录权限。可在设置中关闭声音和触觉反馈。删除 App 及其数据将移除本机存档；设备备份请在系统设置中管理。

购买与联系支持

下载购买由 Apple 处理，我们不接收完整付款卡号。若你主动联系 telegram2ai@gmail.com，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容与自愿提供的附件，用于回复及处理问题。请只提供解决问题所需的信息。可通过该邮箱要求删除支持通信中的个人信息，我们会在适用义务允许的范围内处理。

外部链接和本网站

只有主动选择后，游戏才会打开支持或隐私页面。本网站不含分析脚本或广告。托管服务 GitHub Pages 可能为网站运行和安全处理访问记录。Apple 商店、系统备份与邮件服务遵循各自的隐私政策。

儿童与更新

游戏不主动收集儿童个人信息。请勿在支持邮件中提供不必要的儿童信息。若数据处理方式变化，我们会更新本政策并注明日期。隐私问题请联系 telegram2ai@gmail.com。
