Ride at your own pace

A native, offline 3D cycling game for iPhone and iPad. Ride a bicycle along the coast as a pelican, collect fish, jump, spread your wings and enjoy the changing light. Free to download: unlimited riding on your first day, then 10 free minutes a day, with one optional in-app purchase (“Unlock Full Game”) processed by Apple. No ads and no account.

Controls

Hold the lower-left arrows to steer. Hold the lower-right button to accelerate; the minus button brakes. Use Jump and Wings for tricks, the bell for a ring and the speaker button for a pelican call. The camera button cycles through five views. Drag and pinch in Orbit view.

Auto-fishing takes over after four seconds without steering when enabled. Golden fish are worth five points. In Settings you can choose dawn, sunset or night, adjust graphics and audio, and change the language.

Saves and settings

Your ride, achievements and preferences are saved on your device. Leaving the app pauses the game. A new ride resets its distance and fish score while keeping achievements. There is no cloud sync between devices. Removing the app and its data removes local saves.

18 interface languages

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Arabic, Dutch, Swedish, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai and Indonesian. Choose System default or a language in Settings.

沿着海岸，慢慢骑

为 iPhone 与 iPad 打造的原生离线 3D 骑行游戏。和鹈鹕一起骑过海岸，收集鱼、跳跃、展翅，欣赏清晨、夕阳和星夜。免费下载：第一天不限时长，之后每天免费骑 10 分钟；提供一项由 Apple 处理的可选 App 内购买“解锁完整版”。无广告、无需注册账号。

怎么玩

按住左下角箭头变道，右下角按钮加速，减号按钮刹车。跳跃和展翅可完成特技，铃铛与喇叭可发出声音。相机按钮切换五种镜头，环绕镜头支持拖动和双指缩放。

开启自动追鱼后，停止转向四秒，鹈鹕会自动寻找前方的鱼。金鱼计五分。设置中可选择时间、声音、画质与语言。

进度与语言

骑行进度、成就和设置保存在本机。进入后台会暂停；新旅程重置本次距离和鱼分，保留成就。没有跨设备云同步。删除 App 及其数据将删除本机记录。

支持简体中文、繁体中文、英语、日语、韩语、西班牙语、法语、德语、巴西葡萄牙语、意大利语、俄语、阿拉伯语、荷兰语、瑞典语、土耳其语、越南语、泰语和印度尼西亚语，可跟随系统或手动切换。
